# Research Logic Map (final audit version)

**Central question.** Is the post-2013 swing in Kazakhstan's trade balance with China distinctive to the Kazakhstan–China relationship, and are its distinctive components consistent with the mechanisms through which the BRI was expected to operate, in particular through strategic mineral exports?

**Why the balance.** It is the outcome named in the registered topic and in public debate, and an identity that forces an account of every flow. It is not a welfare measure; the thesis does not treat the deficit as a loss (§1.3, §7.4).

**Logic.** Decompose the swing (identity) → compare it with Kazakhstan's other partners (distinctiveness from Kazakhstan's side) → compare the import part with China's other regional partners (distinctiveness from China's side) → test export components statistically and for timing → match components to BRI mechanisms → grade each claim as consistent / inconsistent / absence of evidence. The two comparisons are separate, not sequential.
