# Research Logic Map (final validated version)

**Central question.** Is the post-2013 change in Kazakhstan–China merchandise trade, summarised by the swing in the bilateral balance, distinctive to the Kazakhstan–China relationship, and is it consistent with the BRI mechanisms that are observable in merchandise trade, in particular through strategic mineral exports?

**Why the balance.** It is the registered outcome and an accounting identity: every export and import change is a component of it, so explaining it requires an account of the whole merchandise relationship. It is not a welfare measure and not the same as the trade relationship; turnover is reported alongside it. Conclusions are limited to merchandise trade.

**Why each component is needed.** Minerals: the registered topic. Agri-food: the one component matching a BRI mechanism in products, and the main test of BRI timing. Financing evidence: the only direct measure of M3 timing. Regional comparisons: the test of whether the largest component is Kazakhstan-specific. Two-sided dependence (§7.6): interprets the mineral result without assuming leverage. Concentration indices (§6.3): descriptive context for composition; not used in conclusions.
