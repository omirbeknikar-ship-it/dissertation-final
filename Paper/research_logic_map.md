# Research Logic Map (revised, third round)

One central question; every chapter and analysis answers a step of it.

**Central question.** How much of the BRI-era deterioration in Kazakhstan's trade balance with China was specific to the bilateral relationship, and does the timing and composition of that bilateral component match the mechanisms through which the BRI was expected to operate, in particular through strategic mineral exports?

**Logic (a funnel).** Total deterioration (15.5 bn) → part not shared with Kazakhstan's other partners (14.3 bn, the upper bound on any China-related cause, BRI included) → part not shared with China's other neighbours either (export side only, net ≈ −0.2 bn) → test each remaining component, and the timing of the regional component, against BRI mechanisms (M1 connectivity/market access; M2 resource finance) → conclude what the BRI can and cannot explain.
