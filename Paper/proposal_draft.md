# Proposal Draft

## Working Title

BRI's Impact on Kazakhstan-China Trade Balance: The Role of Strategic Mineral Exports

## Summary

This project examines whether the Belt and Road Initiative improved Kazakhstan's bilateral trade balance with China and whether strategic mineral exports changed the relationship between BRI and that trade balance. The project begins from a distinction between trade growth and trade-balance improvement. Kazakhstan-China trade has expanded in the post-BRI period, but an increase in total trade does not necessarily mean that Kazakhstan's position improved.

The research is grounded primarily in Dependency Theory and asymmetrical interdependence. Kazakhstan exports resource-based products, while China exports a broader range of manufactured goods and industrial inputs. This structure may reproduce unequal trade relations even when connectivity improves. At the same time, strategic minerals such as uranium and copper-related products may strengthen Kazakhstan's export position because they generate significant export value and are linked to China's industrial and energy priorities.

The project will use annual bilateral trade data from verified public sources. Aggregate trade flows will be used to calculate Kazakhstan's trade balance with China. Commodity-level trade data will be used to measure strategic mineral exports. The empirical strategy will remain parsimonious because annual data produce a small sample. The planned model will estimate the association between the post-BRI period, strategic mineral exports, and Kazakhstan's trade balance, with an interaction term testing whether the mineral export effect changed after BRI.

The expected contribution is to shift the discussion from whether BRI increased Kazakhstan-China trade to whether it improved Kazakhstan's bilateral trade position. The final paper will avoid strong causal claims unless the data and identification strategy support them. At the current stage, this repository documents the research design, planned data sources, theoretical framework, and reproducible analysis workflow.
